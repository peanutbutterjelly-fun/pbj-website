    ■ About PBJ

    Peanut Butter Jelly is a platform where experiences are designed to be curated by artists, found by enthusiasts and indulged in by everyone

    PBJ is a guide to the unexplored crowd and nooks of Mumbai that don't make the usual lists. Think odd corners, creative experiments, a roadmap to undercover artists and people with curated taste. Every week, we discover and present hidden gems worth leaving the house for.

    The goal is to make you know where to come looking after the boredom of a rinse-and-repeat weekend plan leaves you wanting something fresh.

    Who's cooking?

    Meet the team assembling this delicious PBJ sandwich for y'all

    Drishti Gowda

    CEO & founder · mostly goes by Vision

    One whom people hand the talking pillow to, even when she is not ready. Because she has a switch. At least that is what she calls it, a switch that lights up when she has to speak, engage, converse, chat, convince, sell or simply impress anyone in a room. She's known for not being outgoing, but when she is, she's always seen charming people and making new friends as if it's a Pokémon-collecting hobby. In a few words, she is a selective participant who survives on day-round cups of coffee, books she cannot keep down, songs that itch her brain, and people she refers to as "The Twinkle Of Her Eyes".

    Disha Gowda

    CMO & founder

    One who will never talk to you if you make her talk without her heart in it. Talking depletes her, but two things refill her energy: Orange Mocha + Her Passion for Cinematography + Money. She loves money, but her major motivator to make money is the month-end credit card bills she piles up because she never believes she could be broke, like ever. One thing this girl does well is never saying "NO" to a plan. Every new plan includes Disha. OUTGOING for her is self-explanatory. Her committed energy toward her friendships and work is what earns her lasting connections and that enjoyable money. In a few words, she is SORTED.
